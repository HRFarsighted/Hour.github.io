I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Qiao Liu.

My research connects knowledge extraction with spatiotemporal data mining. I study robust and interpretable methods for non-stationary multivariate time series, spatiotemporal heterogeneity, multimodal knowledge disentanglement, and LLM-driven reasoning agents.

I welcome research collaborations on time-series forecasting, knowledge reasoning, and intelligent agents. Please feel free to contact me.
