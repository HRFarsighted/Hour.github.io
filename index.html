Profile

About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Technology at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China, advised by Prof. Qiao Liu.

My work connects knowledge extraction with spatiotemporal data mining. I study robust and interpretable models for multivariate time series and build reasoning systems for network, electromagnetic, and transportation scenarios. I am also interested in LLM-driven reasoning agents.

Questions I work on

Research

Methods for data that changes over time, connects as a graph, and carries meaning in context.

R/01

Spatiotemporal data mining

Robust and interpretable forecasting under non-stationarity and spatiotemporal heterogeneity, using state-space models, graphs, and causal reasoning.

R/02

Knowledge extraction & reasoning

Relation extraction, knowledge-graph reasoning, and multimodal knowledge disentanglement for complex real-world events.

R/03

LLM reasoning agents

LLM-driven agents that combine semantic consistency, structured knowledge, and iterative reasoning for reliable decisions.
